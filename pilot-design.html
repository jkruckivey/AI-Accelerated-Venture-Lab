Pilot design

A contained, practical experiment

The intent is to scope a small pilot that fits within existing programming, uses governed AI access via Nebula One, and yields actionable insights about teaching, learning, and workflow.

Format options

  • Short intensive: multi-day sprint format (e.g., 2–4 days)
  • Course module: embedded within an existing venture course
  • Workshop: a guided studio session with defined deliverables

Final format to be selected during scoping with faculty partners.

Participants and scope

  • Cohort size: team-based; small-to-moderate
  • Work product: prototypes and analyses appropriate to the timebox
  • Constraint: emphasize management skill (delegation + evaluation), not prompt "tricks"

Tools and access

Use Nebula One for governed access to leading AI models with cost controls and compliance considerations.

  • AI-assisted research and synthesis
  • Rapid drafting and iteration of venture artifacts
  • Prototype/service blueprint support (as feasible)

Instructor role

  • Set deliverables and timeboxes
  • Coach teams on scoping and evaluation
  • Surface assumptions and evidence quality
  • Clarify academic integrity and disclosure expectations

Risks and mitigations

  • Over-reliance on AI outputs: require explicit assumptions, evidence checks, and reflection.
  • Variable output quality: teach evaluation routines; include checkpoints and peer review.
  • Governance/appropriate use: use Nebula One; clarify permitted uses and disclosure expectations.
  • Uneven team participation: structured roles (delegator/evaluator/documenter) and brief accountability artifacts.

Success measures (pilot-level)

  • Student outputs: completeness and coherence of artifacts produced within the timebox.
  • Skill signals: evidence of effective delegation, evaluation, and iteration.
  • Faculty feedback: feasibility, workload, and instructional value.
  • Platform insights: observed usage patterns and governance considerations for Nebula One.

Next steps

  • Confirm pilot partner(s) and select a format option.
  • Define deliverables, checkpoints, and disclosure expectations.
  • Draft a lightweight facilitation plan and success measures.
