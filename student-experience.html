Student journey

What students do

The pilot emphasizes management practice: students define objectives, delegate tasks to AI, evaluate output quality, and iterate toward stronger decisions and deliverables.

Stage 1 — Define the opportunity

  • Select a problem space and articulate a clear target customer/user.
  • Define success criteria (what "good" looks like).
  • State key assumptions to test.

Stage 2 — AI-assisted research

  • Rapid market scan, competitor landscape, and positioning options.
  • Draft interview prompts and a lightweight validation plan.
  • Identify gaps, weak claims, and missing evidence.

Stage 3 — Prototype and model

  • Develop a prototype or service blueprint (appropriate to time available).
  • Draft a basic financial outline and pricing hypotheses.
  • Generate alternatives quickly, then select based on criteria.

Stage 4 — Test and iterate

  • Run quick tests (concept tests, messaging tests, scenario checks).
  • Refine the offering and narrative based on evidence.
  • Document what changed and why (management reasoning).

Stage 5 — Pitch-ready deliverables

Students deliver a coherent, evidence-informed venture story.

  • Venture concept + target customer
  • Market and competitor analysis (assumptions clearly stated)
  • Prototype or service model
  • Financial outline + key sensitivities
  • Pitch narrative and recommended next steps

Skills emphasized

  • Delegation: scoping tasks, specifying outputs, setting constraints.
  • Judgment: identifying errors, weak assumptions, or "confident nonsense."
  • Iteration: using fast cycles to improve decisions and artifacts.
  • Communication: clear narratives grounded in evidence.

Internal pilot hub. Student experience is illustrative and will be refined during scoping.
